# Track A — exact finite closed functional age integrated: seven-referee audit

15 November 2026. Seven internal independent mathematical criticism roles, **not** reports from outside referees.

1. **Heredity.** Merged PR #539 proves that restricting a finite canonical C₀ presentation to a complete-unary-closed set preserves *empty-or-minimal strong* component stages, allowing unused index values. PR #544 transports that result to arbitrary finite closed subsets of a countable model with cofinal exact canonical stages. Neither conclusion follows merely from graph-inducedness.

2. **Realization.** Merged PR #548 constructs a single induced complete-functional embedding of every finite canonical C₀ structure into the actual countable limit, with closed and globally strong finite image. The converse of heredity is not deduced from homogeneity or graph-reduct universality alone.

3. **Exact age.** Merged PR #561 combines the two directions with explicit 2-sparsity, an empty-or-minimal component presentation and literal equality of complete unary values. Its exact-head dedicated named theorem/axiom check and whole-repository Lean check passed before merge. The final two-way statement applies to the *unordered finite C-closed functional age*; it is not a statement about the ordered Ramsey expansion.

4. **Unique countable limit.** Merged PR #555 establishes uniqueness of countable complete-functional C₀ models **under both** cofinal canonical-stage and full finite closed-base extension hypotheses. It does not assert uniqueness for an arbitrary countable two-sparse graph, or from closure algebra alone.

5. **Automorphism obstruction.** The finite minimal-strong presentation \(\varnothing\subset\{a\}\subset K_4\) has lower part \(P=\{a\}\), three new vertices, and local automorphism group \(S_3\). No labelled linear order on its new vertices is fixed under that group. Consequently a chosen *ordered local isomorphism type* specifies an orbit, not a pointwise-invariant order. Manuscript draft #179 fixes this ambiguity.

6. **Finite order transport.** Draft Lean PR #588 proposes to certify that two complete-functional transports of the same representative differ by an automorphism fixing their common named lower part and aligning their local orders. Its test examines 36 pairs of the six \(K_4\) local transports. This is **not yet a merged Lean theorem**; the global choice of local orbits and tail-extension lemma remain separate.

7. **Trust and editing scope.** The updated English proof ledger distinguishes exact-head merged results from pending PRs. This documentation change does not alter Lean proof terms, manuscript TeX, historical collaborator-deleted TODOs or the manuscript's PARTIAL marker. No local-order or big Ramsey statement is promoted on the strength of the unordered finite-age result.

**Boundary:** The unordered finite closed functional age, universality, homogeneity and uniqueness under the stated hypotheses are integrated. The local selected-order construction and ordered Ramsey claims remain to be validated independently.
