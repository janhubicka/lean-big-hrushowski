# Track B: adversarial audit of literal birth-labelled source cuts

6 November 2026. Internal role-separated mathematical checks,
not independent outside human referees.

## Exact result

For any finite subset s of the canonical countable C_F birth
carrier and any stage index n, the image of the literal stage-n
source cut under the canonical birth map equals precisely the
subset of s of vertices born before n.

If n≤m and no birth level of s falls into [n,m), then both
source cuts have the same LITERAL birth-labelled image.
This identifies gaps across nonconsecutive represented
levels without inventing an increasing global omega-map
or a relabelling of dependent finite stage carriers.

   original finite source under toVertex. No newly chosen
   finite copy or arbitrary enumeration is introduced.
2. The reverse inclusion uses the verified exact
   stageImage ↔ birthLevel < n and its original
   finite-stage witness; it does not assume that
   arbitrary countable vertices lie in any prescribed stage.
3. The equality holds for ALL finite sources, even ones
   not closed under the auxiliary b-operation. Later
   lifting lemmas must still impose b-closure separately.
4. Empty sources, n=m, and arbitrarily many skipped
   source levels all follow without a nonemptiness premise.
5. Distinct birth labels are never identified. The
   theorem proves equality of sets of actual birth
   vertices, not equality of the dependent stage carrier
   subtypes themselves.
6. The half-open interval uses n≤i<m. An index i=m is
   intentionally NOT excluded, since its vertices are
   absent from both stage-n and stage-m cuts.

   inducedness, d-closure, order and complete b-values
   must be imported independently at the lifting step.
8. The theorem does not prove the finite level assignment
   extends to an increasing self-map of all omega. Such
   an extension may not exist and is not needed here.
