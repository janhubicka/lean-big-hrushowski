# Track B — adversarial audit: literal selected-cut equivalence

1 November 2026. Role-separated internal mathematical scrutiny.

## Endpoint

For any finite b-closed set `s` of actual birth-labelled C_F
vertices, the source cut `stageCut s (n+1)` is **bijective**
to one selected carrier consisting of `stageCut s n` and all
fresh ordinals for the requests selected by s at birth n.
The bijection is the existing literal inclusion
`selectedSourceMap`, not any arbitrary enumeration or a
quotient of request tags. This permits composition of the
genuinely partial old-source batch map with the inverse
equivalence, giving a map defined on the **actual successor cut**.

## Hostile referee checks

1. **Surjectivity:** relies on precise equality of the selected
   carrier image with the complete successor cut; without
   b-closedness, a partial block could break this equality.
2. **Injectivity:** follows from literal old/new disjoint
   summands and exact request-tag + ordinal identities. It
   does not rely on graph inducedness.
3. **Literal labels:** the map sends old selected vertices to
   their literal old vertices and fresh (tag,ordinal) to
   the identical fresh (tag,ordinal), including all nonedges.
   It introduces no competing copy or arbitrary choice.
4. **Empty source:** source and target selected old carriers
   may be empty. There is no need for a total `U_n→U_m`
   outside the selected old cut.
5. **Induction:** `selectedCutOneStepLift` now has domain
   the real cut of the next stage. It takes an induced,
   d-closed old-source transport record, but does not itself
   prove the complete finite induction or uniqueness.
6. **Order:** genuine `stageLinearOrder` is explicitly
   installed to construct the request/ordinal target map,
   not an arbitrary finite order.
7. **Kernel:** four exact axiom endpoints are registered.
   Full CI must pass before any green manuscript marker.
8. **Critical limitation:** the countable birth-stage domain
   is finite at each step; arbitrary finite sources may
   skip intermediate levels. The final induction must
   choose only their represented levels and preserve
   complete old b-values.

These are internal adversarial roles, not external referees.
