# Adversarial audit — support recovery for partial finite embeddings

7 November 2026. Role-separated internal mathematical review,
not claims of independent human referee reports.

## Exact reduction

For a finite source S and old support P and fresh block B, define
their literal subsets inside the ACTUAL selected source subtype.
Given an injective map e:{u∈S}→V, complete value equality
e[(P∪B)∩S]=Q∪C and whole-block equality e[B∩S]=C,
the named old support has image e[P∩S]=Q whenever
P∩B=∅ and Q∩C=∅.

The result invokes the already verified generic
completeBlock_supportImage on the subtype S. No extension
of e to the full source U or to the countable birth carrier
is attempted.

## Independent hostile checks

1. Domain: the embedding acts only on {u∈S}; U may be
   nonempty and V empty when S is empty.
2. Source subsets: the lemma does not assume P,B⊆S.
   Later concrete C_F applications must prove these
   containments using b-closure, and then identify their
   subtype preimages with the original named sets.
3. Complete value: exact ONTO equality of the complete
   support-plus-block image is indispensable. An inclusion
   alone cannot give exact support recovery.
4. Whole fresh block: exact e[B∩S]=C is needed;
   merely sending vertices into C is insufficient.
5. Disjointness: old and fresh pieces are disjoint in
   source and target. The C_F birth-level calculation
   supplies this property; it cannot be omitted.
6. Injection: an old support vertex may otherwise collide
   with a fresh block image and destroy support equality.
7. Literal names: selectedFiniteSet_image_val proves that
   when P⊆S, the selected-subtype set has image exactly
   P under the subtype inclusion; no abstract graph copy.
8. Reuse: no graph/predimension/order/C_F parameter
   appears in the theorem. It applies equally to the
   C_0 component-support argument when the corresponding
   full-value and whole-component facts are available.
9. Scope: exact old-support transport is only one part of
   finite unique level lifting. Fresh size, complete
   ordered local adjacency codes and ordinal positions
   still need recovery from an arbitrary embedding.
10. Kernel: three standalone exact axiom endpoints; full
   CI, placeholder scan and finite regressions must pass.
   No green manuscript marker until then.

These are internal role-separated checks, not independent
outside referees.
