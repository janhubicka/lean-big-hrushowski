# Track A — uniqueness among countable functional canonical C₀ models
14 November 2026

Seven independent *internal* adversarial mathematical referee roles, not external human referee reports.

**Precisely stated claim.** Let (M,CM) and (N,CN) be two countable C₀ functional models. Each admits cofinal literal finite canonical component presentations with exact complete ambient unary values, and each has the full **finite functional extension property over arbitrary finite closed named bases**. Then there is one bijection e:ℕ≃ℕ preserving and reflecting graph adjacency and transporting every entire unary value: CN(e x)=e[CM(x)]. Indeed any finite closed full-functional partial isomorphism extends to such an isomorphism. In particular, every model meeting these hypotheses is isomorphic to the actually constructed limit.

1. **Two-model referee.** The source and target are genuinely independent graphs and complete unary functions. The general finite forth lemma is applied once from M to N and once from N to M on the inverse partial isomorphism. No identification of both models with the same graph reduct, or reuse of the actual limit's self-homogeneity, is assumed.
2. **Extension hypothesis referee.** Each candidate explicitly satisfies `HasClosedBaseFunctionalExtension`, including all finite canonical target structures, complete unary equality, induced graph biconditionals and pointwise fixing of named closed bases. Cofinal finite canonical stages alone are *not* enough for uniqueness.
3. **Closed image referee.** Every intermediate finite map carries C-closed source and target and preserves the entire function. Target strongness follows from the target canonical-stage theorem and complete-functional image closure; it is not assumed for an arbitrary finite induced graph map.
4. **Joint-step referee.** A source step adds k to the source. Apply the same generic forth lemma to its inverse to add k to the target. The already verified `Extends_symm` and `Extends.trans` ensure both requirements survive pointwise, without a new argument about finite component orders.
5. **Infinitary referee.** The existing exact `exists_functional_equiv_extending` of #464 handles the entire countable union. The global bijection is one map preserving *both* graph and complete unary values, not merely separate graph and unary embeddings. Both carriers are exhausted by genuine finite joint steps.
6. **Empty initial map referee.** From cofinal canonical finite stages follows 2-sparsity on every finite set. Therefore the empty finite graph map is globally strong in both models; its source and target are automatically C-closed, and full unary membership is vacuous. This gives a valid initial `FiniteClosedFunctionalPartialIso` without assuming a nonempty finite base.
7. **Kernel/scope referee.** Require exact-head full and dedicated Lean builds, four named theorem axiom certificates, a placeholder scan, and independent finite regressions checking complete-unary transport and joint-step invariance under relabelling. Finite tests are not proofs of the countable result. The theorem is a uniqueness characterization **under explicit canonical-stage and extension hypotheses**, not a claim about every arbitrary countable two-sparse graph. The paper's uniform chosen local ordered representatives for the Ramsey expansion remain completely separate. No TeX or manuscript validation marker is touched.
