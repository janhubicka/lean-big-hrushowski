# Track A — exact finite closed functional age in a countable canonical C₀ model
14 November 2026

Seven distinct internal adversarial referee roles; these are not independent human referees.

**Claim.** If (N,C) admits cofinal literal finite canonical C₀ presentations whose *entire* unary values equal the ambient C, then every finite C-closed set s has an induced finite graph with its own canonical index, every unused level empty and every nonempty level minimal strong, whose entire canonical unary values are exactly C on that same labelled carrier. An arbitrary family of finite C-closed subsets therefore automatically admits finite canonical presentation certificates.

1. **Literal embedding referee.** The enclosing finite stage has carrier T={v∈ℕ : v∈stage t}; its closed restriction has nested carrier U={u∈T : u∈sT}. The actual desired carrier S={v∈ℕ : v∈s} is only canonically *equivalent* to U, not definitionally equal. The proof constructs that exact equivalence and uses it to transport both adjacency and component indices.
2. **Closedness referee.** The restricted set sT is closed under the **entire canonical value** of the enclosing finite stage, because equality with the ambient C and C-closedness of s imply no predecessor leaves s. Merely knowing s⊆stage t would not suffice.
3. **Unused index referee.** The original `HasCanonicalFiniteStages` certificate permits oldStage κ k = oldStage κ(k+1). The restricted index must retain these empty slots. New local heredity #539 handles actual empty-or-minimal stages; do not substitute the obsolete all-minimal hypothesis.
4. **Predimension referee.** `canonicalValue_restrict_emptyOrMinimalSteps` proves the induced finite minimal-strong property. `minimalSteps_relabel` transports it through the *induced* equivalence U≃S. No global strongness of s is required to prove its own finite canonical presentation, and no original index ordering of incomparable components is assumed.
5. **Complete-value referee.** The equality on S is of full labelled finite sets of natural-number vertices, via `canonicalValue_restrict_image` and `canonicalValue_relabel`. The entire canonical operation, not just its immediate neighbours or cardinality, survives.
6. **Independent falsification referee.** Exhaustively test small canonical graphs/indexings and every closed induced subset, *with all permutations of its literal finite carrier*. Confirm empty-or-minimal index stages and full unary values in the independently relabelled graph. Include nonclosed negative controls. Finite testing cannot establish the countable theorem.
7. **Trust and scope referee.** Require dedicated exact-head Lean build, the exact two theorem axiom reports, whole-source placeholder scan and full repository CI after the parent #539 kernel certificate is merged. This is the hereditary direction of the **unordered finite functional age**; a chosen uniform local order for the Ramsey expansion, uniqueness of other countable functional models, and the (C_F) results remain independent. Do not upgrade manuscript validation markers or alter its TeX.
