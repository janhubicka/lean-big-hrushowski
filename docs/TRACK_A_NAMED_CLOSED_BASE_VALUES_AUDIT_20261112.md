# Track A: named finite closed-base complete unary values — adversarial audit

12 November 2026. Seven separate critical reviewer roles; no claim of external human peer review.

**Mathematical scope.** Given a finite C-closed subset A of the countable functional C₀ structure, define F(a) to consist of all *literal members of A* belonging to the entire finite unary value C(a). For an enclosing finite canonical stage S containing A, the natural injection i:A→S transports F(a) *exactly* to the full canonical C-value at i(a). This supplies the previously assumed named-base F in the finite two-sided amalgamation theorem.

1. **Closure reviewer:** Closure of A under all complete C-values is essential. Without it, F(a) images only C(a)∩A, and can omit genuine dependencies. Do not replace closure by graph-inducedness.
2. **Literal carrier reviewer:** Both A and S are finite *subtypes of the same ℕ*, not just abstract finite sets of the same cardinality. The inclusion `closedBaseStageInclusion` fixes original numeric labels and is injective.
3. **Whole function reviewer:** F(a) lists all of C(a) in the literal closed base, not just immediate graph neighbours or the generator's component. Its image is equality of complete finite sets.
4. **Graph reviewer:** The literal injection preserves AND reflects adjacency because both graphs are induced from N. No graph isomorphism or change of coordinates is silently assumed.
5. **Component reviewer:** The enclosing stage may choose an arbitrary canonical chronology. The exact complete unary-value property of that stage suffices. No requirement of equality of numerical component indices with the base presentation is needed.
6. **Finite regression reviewer:** Exhaust all reflexive/transitive unary systems of size at most four; for each closed base and enclosing set verify F(a) maps to exact C(a); for nonclosed sets find counterexamples to the same equality. Independently check induced adjacency and nonadjacency for all small simple graphs.
7. **Trust reviewer:** Four exact theorem endpoints, dedicated Lean kernel check, standard axiom audit, whole-source placeholder scan, and full repository CI are mandatory before merge. The eventual all-closed-base countable extension theorem must also verify right-factor compatibility with F and invoke the separately drafted #451/#453; this theorem alone does not establish functional homogeneity. No manuscript marker is touched.
