# Track A: closed functional joint-step recursion — adversarial proof audit

12 November 2026. Seven independent adversarial review roles, *not* reports by external human referees.

**Theorem scope.** A finite functional partial isomorphism between two countable C₀ structures consists of a finite strong partial **graph** isomorphism, C-closed source and target, and preservation/reflection of membership in the entire complete unary operation. A joint-step hypothesis states that every such finite map extends to another such map covering any prescribed next source AND target vertices. Under exactly that hypothesis, recursion and the already kernel-certified functional union theorem produce a bijection extending the initial finite map which preserves/reflects all graph edges and transports the complete unary operation.

1. **Coherence referee:** The extension relation must be the existing pointwise `FiniteStrongPartialIso.Extends` on the graph maps. Each new finite map separately carries full functional and closedness certificates. No assumption that mere inclusion of domains determines the map is made.
2. **Domain/range referee:** The joint-step premise explicitly demands both prescribed vertices k be in the *next* source and target finite domains. The recursive chain then exhausts ℕ in both directions. A one-sided extension property would not provide surjectivity.
3. **Full-value referee:** The new structure stores the exact unary-membership biconditional for each finite map and closure of both its finite domains. Those hypotheses are exactly the ones required by the previously merged `unionEquiv_completeUnary`; no graph-only back-and-forth theorem is used in place of the functional argument.
4. **Initial-map referee:** At recursive stage zero the chain is literally the specified `p0`, and the certified `union_at_stage` proves that the limit bijection extends `p0` pointwise. This is essential for strong homogeneity, rather than merely existence of some automorphism.
5. **Limit referee:** The limit graph equivalence is precisely `unionEquiv` from the existing `StrongBackAndForthLimit`, not a different choice of bijection. Graph adjacency and nonadjacency are both reflected.
6. **Counterexample referee:** Dropping closedness lets a finite unary map omit a true complete dependency outside its domain; dropping the full membership requirement lets a finite graph isomorphism fail on unary values. Independent finite regression includes both negative controls and all valid finite component chains under relabellings.
7. **Trust and manuscript referee:** Seven exact `#print axioms` endpoints, dedicated Lean kernel build, full repo CI and source placeholder scan are mandatory. The finite joint-step theorem **itself remains open**; the present result is an implication from that precise premise to functional homogeneity and uniqueness. No manuscript TeX/green marker or chosen local ordered representative is changed.

**Next task:** Construct the finite closed functional joint-step operation for the actual countable functional limit using the already drafted arbitrary-closed-base extension #459, with a common closed enlargement covering the next source and target vertex. Show that the new finite image is again C-closed from *exact equality of the full unary functions*, and that the induced finite partial map remains globally strong via the earlier closed-set strongness lemma. Then this conditional homogeneity theorem applies without more infinitary construction.
