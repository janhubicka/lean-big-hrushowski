## Six adversarial mathematical checks

1. **Literal old vertices.** The split map sends each
old subtype x to the *same vertex* x.val in V. The
stage response is required to agree with the prescribed
birth-labelled e pointwise, not just modulo an abstract
isomorphism of the source.

2. **Complete induced graph.** The split representation
preserves and reflects *every* adjacency, including all
missing old/fresh and fresh/fresh edges. Positive-edge
homomorphisms are insufficient for C_F growth or
strong/strict predimension calculations.

3. **Minimal strictness.** The input is a proper
IsMinimalDClosedStep a (a∪b). Its image in the split
graph is strictly minimal, not merely weakly strong.
The fresh block cannot be empty under this hypothesis.

4. **Named canonical support.** The assembled response
extracts P=cl_A^d(N(b)∩A), proves P≤d A and exact
support, and maps it to the *actual* f[P] in U_n.
It cannot silently choose a different isomorphic support.

5. **No circularity.** The proof uses only the finite
tagged construction and its certified response lemmas,
not the Fraïssé extension property of M which is being
established. The countable carrier and its globally
d-closed finite stages are constructed independently.

6. **Remaining global theorem.** This result returns a
response on the split carrier a_subtype⊕Fin(|b|).
The original arbitrary labelled target a∪b is equivalent
to that split carrier. The resulting map must be
transported through that equivalence and finite
minimal-step decomposition composed to obtain the
full d-closed extension property for *arbitrary*
