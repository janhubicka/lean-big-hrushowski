## Claim and improvement
Let s be a finite source of the actual countable C_F birth-labelled
universe, and let e be a map whose domain is *only* the selected
subtype of s. Assume e takes the chosen source fresh vertex to the
chosen target fresh vertex, transports its entire b-value ONTO the
entire target b-value, and preserves AND REFLECTS the intrinsic
birth-level preorder on s. Then e maps the source named old support
onto the target named old support and the source WHOLE fresh block
onto the target WHOLE fresh block.

This discharges BOTH explicit level-separation premises of the
general partition theorem in #386 from a genuine finite embedding
property; unlike #373, there is no separate whole-block
surjectivity premise. Injectivity is not used by this particular
set-theoretic argument.

## Hostile referee roles
1. **Preorder:** preservation alone is insufficient to infer strict
   old-before-new comparisons. Reflection rules out collapsing two
   distinct birth levels.
2. **Pointed image:** knowing the target fresh vertex (and hence its
   target level m) is essential; no global function of levels is used.
3. **Old source:** every named support member has source birth level
   strictly below n; otherwise the old-labelled claim fails.
4. **Fresh source:** every member of the complete source block has
   birth index exactly n; the preorder therefore forces index m
   for each image.
5. **Complete value:** b(v) is the union of the entire old support
   and complete fresh block. A proper inclusion is insufficient.
6. **Onto:** union image equality and level separation yield BOTH
   separate surjections, not just subset inclusions.
7. **Partial domain:** the source remains a genuine finite subtype,
   with no arbitrary extension to a global self-map.
8. **No hidden closure:** the selected-set formulation needs no
   b-closedness once complete value-image equality is explicitly
   assumed; applying it to a functional embedding of a finite
   b-closed source is the next step.
9. **No overclaim:** this recovers two subsets of a target birth
   value. It does not yet recover the full ordered adjacency code,
   finite ordinal and literal target request tag, or iterate
   the entire finite lifting induction.
10. **Kernel:** exact axiom check is standalone. Merge only upon
