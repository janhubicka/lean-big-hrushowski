# Adversarial audit: equality of C_F b and (R,H) closed substructures

20 October 2026. Main claim: on any subset X of the literal countable C_F birth carrier, possibly infinite, closure under the complete auxiliary b-values is equivalent to closure under each R(v) and all **defined** H-values on finite subsets of actual block roots.

## Roles of hostile review

1. **Infinite-set referee.** Do not assign a numerical predimension to X. Intersect X with a finite birth stage U_n, obtaining a finite b-closed set. This uses the strict birth-level inequalities for old supports and equality of birth indices within a block.
2. **Finite generated closure referee.** The least finite b-closed hull of s⊆X is contained in U_n∩X: it is least among *all* finite b-closed supersets, not merely those in some fixed graph.
3. **Global hull referee.** Finite b-closed sets are globally d-closed, so H(s) is contained in the finite b-closure of s. Thus arbitrary b-closed X contains H(s) for each finite s⊆X, even before restricting s to roots.
4. **Domain referee.** H is *partial*: its permitted argument sets consist exactly of genuine first vertices, represented by rootOf(r)=r. The proof never enlarges the declared domain in the definition of (R,H)-closedness.
5. **Root referee.** Every member of R(v) has rootOf(r)=r; this follows from literal tag/ordinal zero and idempotence of rootOf, not a choice of an isomorphic block.
6. **Converse referee.** If X is R,H-closed, v∈X implies R(v)⊆X. Because R(v) lies in H's domain, H(R(v))⊆X. The fully verified equality H(R(v))=C(v) then forces the complete b-value of v into X.
7. **Empty input referee.** The statements include empty root sets and empty closed vertex subsets. No nonempty-old-base or arbitrary choice of a root is assumed.
8. **Embedding referee.** Equality of closed vertex subsets does **not** establish equality of embeddings: one still must prove complete block/support-value transport, preservation and reflection of H-domains, and the full finite H-value image equation.
9. **Lean referee.** Every new theorem is recorded in CheckAxioms and must pass an integrated build, standard-axiom/placeholder scan, and downstream regressions before a manuscript GREEN marker can be attached. Successful prior dependency CI does not suffice.

The nine reviews are independent mathematical angles considered internally, not claims of spawning external human referees.
