# Adversarial audit — complete root recovery on the C_F birth graph

19 October 2026

## Precise statement

For every **finite** set S closed under the auxiliary b-operation on the actual tagged birth carrier, let R(S) be the first vertex (fresh ordinal 0) of every birth block meeting S. Let H(T) denote the least globally strictly d-closed finite hull of T. Then

  H(R(S)) = S.

The upper inclusion holds because R(S) is a subset of S and S is globally strictly d-closed. The lower inclusion follows by strong induction on birth level n: for a selected block born at n, its actual old support has birth level < n and belongs to S by b-closedness, so the induction hypothesis puts the support in H(R(S)). The first vertex of that block belongs to R(S), hence also to H(R(S)). The actual local strict minimal extension on the birth carrier and the common one-root forcing lemma put the entire fresh block into H(R(S)). This never constructs an infinite predimension.

Defining C(v) as the least finite b-closed superset of {v}, and R(v)=R(C(v)), yields **H(R(v))=C(v)** literally, with all vertices and functions on the actual countable birth graph.

## Adversarial checks

1. **Global closure direction:** For H(R(S)) ⊆ S, S must be globally strictly d-closed, not merely strong or closed inside a chosen stage. This is exact Lean theorem #194.
2. **Finite generation:** C(v) is the least finite b-closed superset from #195. No explicit infinite transitive closure needs to be computed, and arbitrary finite sets of generators remain finite.
3. **Actual root:** The root is the first ordinal of the *same request*, not the first vertex of an isomorphic local type. `OrderedLocalType.fresh_pos` ensures it exists.
4. **Support dependency:** The old support is literal and its birth index is strictly below that of the fresh block. The induction is on the natural birth level, not on a guessed closure depth.
5. **Minimal interval:** The forcing step invokes the transported **minimal strictly d-closed** local interval, preserving all edges and nonedges through the induced stage map. The old-to-new support bound alone is insufficient.
6. **Ambient:** The globally d-closed H-set is restricted to its union with the finite local interval. Root forcing requires no assumption that the finite local interval is itself globally closed.
7. **Nonempty root set negative control:** Omitting one requested block's first vertex invalidates the forcing step; in particular a singleton empty-support block is not generated by an empty set of roots.
8. **Scope of functional equivalence:** This proof establishes the **set-valued equality** H(R(v))=C(v) but does not yet prove that an arbitrary (R,H)-embedding preserves complete local block-support values b(v) (including function domains and the reflexion of nonedges). That equivalence remains separately TODO and cannot be marked GREEN from this theorem alone.
9. **Kernel assurance:** The branch must pass a clean integrated build and exact `#print axioms` and no-placeholder checks for the birth-level induction; role-separated hostile reviews are internal, not external referees.

The proof avoids category-theoretic language and leaves the manuscript's definitions unchanged. The only anticipated text adjustment is to replace the unverified root-recovery *equation* marker after CI success while retaining a TODO for the embedding-equivalence clause.
