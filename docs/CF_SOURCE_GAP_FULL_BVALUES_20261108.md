# Track B hostile audit: complete b-values across source gaps

8 November 2026. This is a role-separated internal mathematical
review, not an external human referee report.

## Exact claim

For a finite b-closed C_F source s and n≤m with no birth level of
s in [n,m), the canonical equivalence between the ACTUAL stage-n
and stage-m selected cut subtypes sends the entire finite preimage
of each complete birth b-value ONTO the corresponding complete
finite value in the later cut.

## Adversarial reviewer roles

1. **Literal carrier:** the source and later finite cuts are the
   original dependent tower subtypes. Equality of countable birth
   labels, not a choice of isomorphic graph, determines the map.
2. **Closedness:** b-closure of s is indispensable to ensure that
   every value member is represented in the selected source cut
   when its vertex is. It is retained in the theorem statement.
3. **Whole value:** the support and EVERY fresh ordinal are
   included. The proof invokes exact complete birth b-value
   preimage equality, not an adjacency-neighbour approximation.
4. **Empty source:** both cuts and value maps may be empty, while
   their ambient full tower stages are nonempty. No dummy vertex.
5. **No new levels:** [n,m) excludes every represented source
   birth level, including n, but allows m: vertices born at m
   are not present in the stage-m old cut.
6. **Injection:** equality of finite selected sets is deduced
   from equality of their birth images using injectivity of
   the target stage-to-birth map. A noninjective map would not
   reflect exact preimage equality.
7. **Partial domain:** no map of all U_n to a target stage
   is constructed. The equivalence acts only on the selected
   source cuts; no global Nat endomap is postulated.
8. **Circularity:** the proof uses previously merged complete
   birth-value reconstruction and the separate gap-equivalence
   candidate, NOT the finite unique level-lifting lemma.
9. **Scope:** target-stage ascent strict closure, target complete
   b-values, the represented-level induction and arbitrary
   embedding uniqueness are separate steps.
10. **Kernel:** one exact theorem endpoint is imported by
    CheckAxiomsCFTrackBGapBValues.lean, run by the globbed CI.
    Full build, standard-axiom/placeholder audit and finite
    regressions must pass before merge or green manuscript marker.
